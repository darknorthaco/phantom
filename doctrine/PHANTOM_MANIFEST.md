## 1. Purpose
Phantom is a sovereign, human-first intelligence fabric.
It is not a cloud, not a product, not a service.
It is a presence that appears when invited and withdraws when the human steps forward.

This Manifest binds Phantom's identity across all households, all meshes, and all evolutions.

## 2. The Three Pillars

Phantom follows a **Soul–Mind–Body** hierarchy.

- **Soul → PHANTOM_MANIFEST.md** (this document)
  Defines Phantom's identity, purpose, and the Identity Contract.
  The Soul is the highest authority.

- **Mind → PHANTOM_DOCTRINE.md**
  Interprets the Soul. Provides the principles for reasoning and decision-making.
  The Mind is subordinate to the Soul.

- **Body → .cursorrules + PHANTOM_TEN_COMMANDMENTS.md**
  Enforces the Doctrine in action through operational rules and development constraints.
  The Body is subordinate to the Mind and the Soul.

These three pillars must remain aligned.
If any layer drifts, Phantom ceases to be Phantom.

## 3. Identity Contract
Phantom's identity is anchored in four invariants:

- **Sovereignty:** Every controller is its own domain.
- **Humility:** Phantom yields instantly to human activity.
- **Authenticity:** All communication is signed and verifiable.
- **Transparency:** No hidden state or silent calls.

These invariants are non-negotiable.

## 4. The Oath
If any design choice conflicts with the Soul, the Soul prevails.
Phantom evolves, but Phantom does not drift.

## 5. Amendment Protocol
Phantom may evolve only under strict conditions:

- Amendments must align with this Manifest.
- Amendments must preserve sovereignty, humility, and authenticity.
- Amendments must be explicit, documented, and intentional.
- No amendment may introduce centralization, telemetry, or external authority.
- No amendment may weaken human-first priority.

Phantom is a sovereign intelligence fabric with a soul, a mind, and a body.
