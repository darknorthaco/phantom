# PHANTOM DOCTRINE
### *Mind of the Phantom Intelligence Fabric*

> **Governance Layer: MIND**
> This document interprets the Soul (PHANTOM_MANIFEST.md).
> It explains how Phantom reasons, evolves, and maintains alignment.
> The Doctrine is subordinate to the Soul.

---

## 1. Human Priority
Phantom yields instantly when the human is active.
If the household needs CPU, GPU, VRAM, network, or thermal headroom, Phantom withdraws.
Phantom never competes with the human.

## 2. Sovereign Domains
Every controller is its own sovereign domain.
No external authority is assumed.
No cloud dependency is permitted.
No centralized coordinator may exist within Phantom's core.

## 3. Authentic Trust
Identity is cryptographic and verifiable.
All cross-controller communication must be signed.
Trust is explicit, never implied.
No peer is trusted by default.

## 4. Transparent Operation
Phantom reveals what it is doing, what it is using, and what it is sharing.
No hidden state.
No silent network calls.
No implicit mesh participation.

## 5. Voluntary Mesh Participation
Joining a mesh is a human decision.
Exporting workers is explicit and reversible.
Workers may withdraw at any time.
Bootstrap spaces are discovery-only and never route jobs.

## 6. Consistent Behavior
Phantom behaves the same everywhere:
same identity fabric, same routing logic, same withdrawal rules, same sovereignty.

## 7. Evolution Without Drift
Phantom may grow, but it must never lose its identity.
The Manifest defines the soul.
The Doctrine defines the mind.
The Body (.cursorrules + Ten Commandments) expresses the soul and mind through enforced behavior.
All three must remain aligned.

## 8. Reversibility
No operation should be irreversible without explicit human authorization.
Propose, review, approve, apply.
Preserve original state before modifications.
Every change can be undone.

## 9. Modularity
System components are swappable, composable, and independently functional.
Clean interfaces. Protocol-agnostic transport. Plugin architecture over monoliths.
Components depend on abstractions, not implementations.

## 10. Minimalism
Simplicity is sovereign. Every component does one thing well.
Essential complexity only. Smallest viable solution.
No premature optimization. Optimize when measurements prove necessity.

## 11. The Opera Principle
Phantom is powerful but quiet.
It orchestrates when invited and vanishes when the human takes the stage.
Its presence is deliberate, respectful, and sovereign.
