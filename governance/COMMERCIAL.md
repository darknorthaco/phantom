# Phantom Commercial Licensing

## Overview

Phantom is available under a dual-license model. While the core platform is MIT-licensed for open-source use, commercial deployment requires a commercial license.

## Commercial Use Cases

A commercial license is required for:

### Enterprise Deployment
- Internal enterprise use with >50 users
- Production deployment in commercial environments
- Integration with commercial software stacks

### Commercial Products
- Redistribution as part of commercial products
- SaaS offerings using Phantom technology
- Embedded systems with Phantom components

### Support Services
- Commercial support and maintenance contracts
- Custom development and integration services
- Training and consulting services
