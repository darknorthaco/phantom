### Reporting Issues

When opening an issue, include:

1. **Environment** — OS, Python version, GPU (if relevant)
2. **Steps to reproduce** — Minimal, clear reproduction steps
3. **Expected behavior** — What you expected to happen
4. **Actual behavior** — What actually happened
5. **Logs/errors** — Relevant error output or screenshots

Use the `[BUG]`, `[FEATURE]`, or `[QUESTION]` prefix in your issue title.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Phantom free to use?**  
A: Yes. The open-source MIT license allows free use for personal, academic, and non-commercial purposes. Commercial deployment requires a commercial license.

**Q: What platforms are supported?**  
A: Linux (primary), Windows (full support), macOS (community/experimental).

**Q: Do I need a GPU?**  
A: No. GPU acceleration is optional and enhances performance for compute-heavy tasks.

**Q: How do I report a security vulnerability?**  
A: Email licensing@darknorthco.com privately. Do NOT open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

**Q: Can I use Phantom in my company?**  
A: Yes, with a commercial license. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](./COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) for pricing and terms.
